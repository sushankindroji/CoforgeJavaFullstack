HTML5 Semantic Tags Demonstration

This page demonstrates commonly used HTML5 semantic elements with examples.

<header>

Purpose: Defines a header for a document, section, or article.

<nav>

Home About Services Contact

Used to declare a block of navigation links.

<section>

Defines a thematic group of related content, usually with a heading.

<article>

Represents an independent, self-contained piece of content such as a blog post, news article, or forum post.

<aside>

Used for content related to the main content but placed separately, such as sidebars, advertisements, quick links, or tips.

<footer>

Defines the footer of a document, section, or article.
